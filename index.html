About Us

The IEEE Global Robotics Championship Malta 2026 is an international student robotics competition organised under IEEE Region 8 Student Activities. Teams design and build robotic systems to complete a series of engineering and strategy-based challenges under competitive conditions.

Genesis is a team of engineering students from the University of Malta. Building on the experience gained from our previous robot, in which we won 4th place, we are developing Genesis with the goal of achieving an even stronger result in the 2026 championship.
