GENESIS / A NEW BEGINNING

Engineering comes to life when you build something that has to work.

Genesis is a team of engineering students from the University of Malta. We’re bringing our learning into the workshop through robot design, development and testing.

Our previous robot achieved fourth place overall in 2024. Now we’re building on that experience to develop Genesis, with the ambition of an even stronger result at the 2026 championship.

THE ARENA / EXPLORE THE FOUR GAMES

01

Time of Fall

A head-to-head challenge to pull the opposing robot off the arena’s edge in the shortest time.

02

Climate CHAOS

Navigate a maze representing a building collapsed after a climate-related disaster.

03

Weight in Line

Collect coloured weights and position them in the designated scoring areas.

04

Hanging Hazard

Score rugby balls while using strategy and manoeuvrability against an opponent.

Game summaries from the IEEE Malta Section. See the official website for competition details and regulations.
